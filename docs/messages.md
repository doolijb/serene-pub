# Messages

**Messages** lets the people who share a pub write to each other privately, one to one or in a group, without leaving the app. A message can carry a session, a character, a lorebook or a file, as long as everyone in the conversation is allowed to see it.

:::tip What it's for
- Arranging a group session: "Can you both make Saturday?" with the session itself attached.
- Passing a character or a lorebook to a friend, read-only, so they can copy it.
- Sending a picture or a file to one person without putting it in a story.
:::

## The basics

Messages needs [user accounts](./users-and-accounts.md) turned on: on a pub with no accounts there is nobody to write to. Once accounts are on, a **Messages** icon appears on the rail for everyone. An administrator can turn the feature off for the whole pub in **Admin › General** under **Messages between people**; the icon then goes for everyone and nothing is lost.

Your messages are private from the other people on the pub, administrators included. They are **not** encrypted: whoever runs the server can read everything stored on it.

## Starting a conversation

1. Open **Messages** on the rail and press **New conversation**.
2. Pick one person for a private conversation, or several for a group. A group can have a **Name**; leave it empty and it is named after the people in it.
3. Write in the field at the bottom and press **Send** (Enter on a keyboard; Shift+Enter starts a new line).

:::tip You should see
The conversation appears at the top of the list under **Unread** or **Recent**, with the faces of the people in it, the last line written and when.
:::

There is only ever one private conversation between two people. Starting a new one with someone you already write to opens the conversation you have.

## Reading

The list on the left groups conversations into **Unread**, **Recent** and **Older**. A small dot before the time marks one with messages you have not seen. Click a conversation to read it: oldest at the top, newest at the bottom, with **Load earlier** at the top when there is more.

A conversation counts as read once you have had it open and in front of you for a moment. The **Messages** icon carries a dot while anything is unread, and a new message also lands in [Activity](./getting-around.md#activity) under **Waiting on you**, unless you are already looking at that conversation. On a phone the dot shows on the **Views** button.

Press **⋯** on a conversation for **Mute** (no more notifications from it; the list still shows new messages), **Rename** and **Add people** (groups), and **Leave**. In a group, the person who started it is its host: only they can rename it, add people and remove them. If the host leaves, the person who has been in the group longest becomes host.

Your own messages have a **⋯** with **Edit** and **Remove**. A removed message leaves a short *Message removed* line so the conversation keeps its shape.

## Attaching things

Press **Attach** under the field to add something to a message:

- **Files**: pictures and documents from your computer or phone, the same kinds and sizes a session composer takes. A picture shows in the conversation; anything else is a card with a download.
- **Sessions**, **Characters**, **Lorebooks**: things of yours, or shared with you, that **everyone in the conversation can already see**.

The picker only offers what passes that test. If someone is added to a group later and can't see something attached earlier, they see *Not shared with you* in its place; the words around it are still there for them. If the thing has since been deleted, everyone sees *No longer available*.

Attaching never shares anything by itself. What people can see is decided elsewhere:

- A **session** is visible to its owner and its guests, and only to them. To attach a session that someone in the conversation isn't part of, add them as a guest first, in the session's settings (see [Group sessions](./group-sessions.md)). The message that refuses tells you who to add.
- A **character** is visible to its owner, to anyone in a session where it is in the cast, and to anyone it has been **shared** with (see [Sharing a character](./characters.md#sharing-a-character)).
- A **lorebook** is visible to its owner and to anyone it has been **shared** with (see [Sharing a lorebook](./lorebooks.md#sharing-a-lorebook)).

Clicking an attached session opens it; a character or lorebook opens in its own view, read-only unless it is yours.

## People

Click a person's face or name anywhere in Messages to see their page: their name, a **Message** button that opens your private conversation with them, and tabs for their **Sessions**, **Characters** and **Lorebooks**, showing only the ones you are allowed to see: sessions you are in with them, characters they have shared with you or that are in a shared session's cast, and lorebooks they have shared with you. Click any row to open it.

## Admins

**Admin › General › Messages between people** turns the feature on or off for the pub. It is on by default once accounts are on. There is no administrator view of other people's conversations.
