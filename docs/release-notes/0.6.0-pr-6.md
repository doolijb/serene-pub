# Serene Pub 0.6.0-pr-6 release notes

A preview of 0.6, released in October 2026. It is about what a session remembers: where its world is kept, what Back can still take back, and how a session ends. Everything in [0.6.0-pr-1](./0.6.0-pr-1.md) still applies; read those notes first if you're coming from 0.5.3.

> **This is a preview build.** Your data comes across by itself, and a backup is taken before anything changes.

## New

- **Every session has its own notes from the start.** A session keeps the world it plays out (places, people, its clock, its scenes and summaries) in its **Session notes**, even with no lorebook. They are listed in **Lorebooks** under **Session notes**, never in a picker. A new session's notes use the Gregorian calendar with no clock set until you or a pipeline set one. See [Your session's own notes](../lorebooks.md).
- **Share your notes.** **Share as a lorebook** turns a session's notes into a lorebook of your own. When the notes sit on top of a lorebook the session reads, you can **Share to ‹book›** (write their changes into that book, all at once) or **Clone with changes and attach** (copy the book with the changes and read the copy instead; the original is never touched).
- **Archive a session.** **Archive** in the session's **⋯** menu makes it read-only: the composer closes, Back, Forward and Regenerate are greyed, routines stop, and it moves to a new **Archived** group at the end of your sessions list. **Unarchive** puts it back.
- **Close out.** **Close out** in the same menu turns what was played since the last scene into a closing scene, summarized and held for your review before it reaches the lorebook. Archiving runs it once. It never runs by itself.
- **Catch up.** When you start a session on the same lorebook as an older one whose last lines never reached the lorebook, the header offers **Catch up on ‹session›**.
- **See what is in the lorebook.** The conversation marks where the story is kept: *In the lorebook up to here* sits after the newest line a saved summary or an applied scene summary was written from. In **Chronicle**, an applied scene says *Written from the story up to ‹time›*.
- **Story notices when the scene moves on.** **Look back over the story** has a new switch, **Notice when the scene moves on** (on): it looks over the lines since its last look and, when the story has moved to a new scene, suggests one at that line with its reason (or begins it, when the session's scenes are Automatic). It is one more question in the same call, asked only when no other trigger fired.
- **Share and Close out say what happened.** **Share to ‹book›** with nothing waiting says *Nothing to share*; **Close out** says *Closed out* and how many closing scenes are being summarized for you.

## Changed

- **Regenerate the last turn is in the header** in the Lair and the Dungeon Crawler, beside Back and Forward, for the session's owner. It takes the whole turn back the way Back does and plays it again; with **On revision: Branch** it branches first.
- **Back stops at what is in the lorebook.** Once you apply a summary (or a scene's summary), the story up to the newest line it was written from is kept, and Back says so.
- **Routines summarize older lines only.** A summary that runs on a schedule never reaches the newest 20 messages, so it doesn't summarize a scene that is still in flux. Summaries you ask for, and Close out, have no such limit.
- **A genre's own summarizers run.** World and character summaries now run the summarizer the session's genre (or preset) chooses, as scene summaries already did.
- **Back, Forward and Regenerate wait for the whole reply.** They stay grey while a reply or an action is still running, including while the reply files its notes, not only while its text arrives.
- An archived session's composer reads *This session is archived. Its owner can unarchive it to play on.*, without *Waiting:*.

## Fixed

- After Back, Forward or a change of seats, other members' pages no longer lose the older messages they had loaded.
- Back now also takes back a scene boundary that started or ended on a line it removes, and Forward puts it back.
- Saving a session's settings no longer overwrites its drafts.
- Opening a session no longer sends the turn order to every member, twice.
- **Share to ‹book›** no longer drops an entry's parent when the parent is already in that book.
- Summarize to Lorebook, starting a history entry, Chronicle and Places use the session's notes when the session reads no lorebook (they used to ask for one). Summarize says *Saving to: Session notes*.
- After switching from one session to another, avatars no longer show *?*.

## For plugin authors

The SDK packages are at `0.6.0-pr-6` on npm (under the `next` tag). New: the lore outlets take `sources` (the messages a write was generated from), `match` and `bind`; registrations take `minAge`; a channel declares its `lore` role; a genre may declare `history.settle: 'on-write'`; a `migrate(fromVersion)` lifecycle moment for annex owners; the events `session-archived`, `scene-summary-applied` and `history-settled`; the `fileLore` and `rememberNote` builders and `closeOutAction` in core-catalog; and conformance law C39. A new guide, *Writing lore from a pipeline*, covers them.

**Breaking: plugin row storage is gone.** `ctx.storage` keeps files only (`ctx.storage.files`, plus `usage()`); `get`, `put`, `query`, `keys`, `delete` and `deleteAll` are removed, with `RowQuery`, `RowEntry`, `RowPage` and `rowQuotaFor`. Keep hidden state in files and session state in an annex field (see the *Storage* guide). Also new: the outlet `core:outlet/propose-scene-cue@1` (*Suggest a new scene*), the sweep duty `sceneMovedOn`, `RUN_KINDS` / `RunKind` on run-status frames, `SessionDossierV1.settledThrough`, `HistorySceneV1.loreSources` and `ComposerClosureV1.waits`.
