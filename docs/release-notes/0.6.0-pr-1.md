# Serene Pub 0.6.0-pr-1 release notes

The first preview of Serene Pub 0.6, released in October 2026.

> **This is a preview build.** It's for trying 0.6 early and telling us what breaks. Your 0.5.3 data comes across by itself, and a backup is taken before anything changes, but keep that backup until 0.6.0 itself is out. A preview never updates itself: to move to the next one, download it yourself (see [Known issues and limits](#known-issues-and-limits)).

## What's new in 0.6

0.6 is the biggest change since Serene Pub began. Most of the app was rebuilt so that every part of a reply can be seen and changed, and so that stories can grow larger without falling apart. What 0.5.3 did is still here, mostly in new places; [Upgrading from 0.5.3](#upgrading-from-053) says where.

### Sessions, and genres to play them in

Chats are now **sessions**, and every session has a **genre**: the kind of session you're playing.

- **Chat**: talk with one or more characters. The default, and the lightest: one request to the model per reply.
- **Adventure**: a narrator describes a world of places, items and stats, and your cast speak for themselves.
- **The Lair**: you are the dungeon. You build the rooms; an AI party explores them.
- **Guide**: ask how to do something in Serene Pub. It answers from the documentation and says which page it used.

See [Genres](../genres.md) and [Sessions](../sessions.md).

### See how every reply is made

Every reply, narration, summary and picture is made by a **pipeline**: a series of steps that gathers the characters, lore and conversation, builds the prompt, calls the model and saves the result. Administrators can open any reply in the **run inspector** to see the exact prompt and which lore went in, so "why did it forget that?" has an answer. Each step's prompt and model can be changed, and a step can be set to wait for your approval before it saves. Chat's new default reply prompt is **Roleplay - Living Scene**. See [Pipelines](../pipelines.md).

### Arrange the session screen

Put **widgets** around the conversation: scene portraits, stats, the lore that was read, the author's note. Save the arrangement as a layout and reuse it. Messages come in five styles (Stage, Bubbles, Novel, Compact and Dreamlit Cameo) and four composers (Classic card, Single-line pill, Tall editor and Page line), and you can write your own style in CSS. Characters can carry **sprites**, such as a set of emotion pictures, that show in the scene. See [Session layout](../session-layout.md).

### Lorebooks that know when, who and where

Lorebooks were rebuilt around three new ideas, all optional:

- **Story time**: date what happened, read your book as it stood at any point in the story, write a change that only starts on a date, and fork a second line of the story to try something else. See [Story time](../lorebook-time.md).
- **Cast**: everyone your world holds, with lore only they know and relationships to each other, drawn as a graph. See [Cast](../lorebook-cast.md).
- **Places**: rooms, towns and roads joined by ways, drawn on a map, holding the things that lie there. See [Places](../lorebook-places.md).

**Loose ends** lists what's left to finish in a book, one fix at a time, and you choose whether your sessions may write to your lorebooks on their own, only with your review, or not at all. See [Lorebooks](../lorebooks.md).

### Images and files in your messages

Attach pictures, text files and PDFs to a message with the paperclip, by dragging them onto the composer or by pasting a screenshot. Models that can see images read them; **What can be attached**, in the composer's **More** (⋮) menu, says what the current model can read. Location and camera details are removed from photos as they upload. See [Attach images and files](../sessions.md#attach-images-and-files).

### The author's note is back

Chat sessions have an **author's note**: your own note to the model, such as what is true right now or where the story should go. Edit it in the session's settings or in its own widget. It's placed at the end of the prompt by default, and SillyTavern chats bring theirs along. See [Author's note](../sessions.md#authors-note).

### Reasoning models

A model that reasons before it answers now streams that reasoning straight into the reply's **Reasoning** fold, and the status says *Wren is reasoning…* until the reply itself begins. How much a model reasons is a setting in its sampling config. What 0.5 called "thinking" is called **reasoning** everywhere now. See [Reasoning](../connections.md#reasoning).

### Connections, rebuilt

- The **Connections** view lists your connections, with which model each job uses (chat, images, embeddings) at the top. States are plain words: *Ready*, *Needs a key*, *Not working*.
- The **model finder** suggests models that fit your computer's memory, and downloads them for you.
- **KoboldCPP, run by Serene Pub** downloads, starts and switches models in the app, draws pictures from the same connection, and can load a vision projector so a local model can read images. **Ollama** can be managed the same way.
- Hosted services include OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter and many more OpenAI-compatible presets.
- A slow model is no longer cut off mid-reply: Serene Pub waits as long as the model keeps working, and gives up only when it sends nothing for 10 minutes. Local servers reuse more of their work between turns, so replies start sooner.

See [Connect a model](../connect-a-model.md) and [Connections](../connections.md).

### A desktop app that updates itself

On Windows, macOS and Linux, Serene Pub now starts from a small launcher: double-click **Serene Pub**, and it puts an icon in the tray and opens in its own window. In regular releases, **Admin › Updates** downloads a new version and restarts on it, and puts the old one back by itself if the new one doesn't start. Serene Pub also backs up your data once a day by default. See [Install](../install.md) and [Updating](../updating.md).

### Admin, help and everything around them

- **Admin** is one area with lists and forms for everything an administrator manages, a **History** of changes, and a **support report** you can paste into a bug report (secrets and names removed). See [System settings](../system-settings.md).
- **Help** is the whole documentation inside the app, searchable, and **Jump** (**Ctrl K**, **⌘K** on a Mac) finds anything you can open. See [Getting around](../getting-around.md).
- **Activity** collects your notifications, such as your turn coming round in a group session or a character's question waiting for your answer.
- Reach your pub from anywhere with the built-in **tunnel**, and protect accounts with **two-factor authentication**. See [Hosting](../hosting.md) and [Users and accounts](../users-and-accounts.md).
- The interface can show in 28 languages, filled in by Google Translate or your own LibreTranslate server once an administrator turns translation on. Your stories are never translated. See [Languages](../languages.md).

### Plugins and the SDK (preview)

0.6 can run **plugins**, which add genres, actions, widgets and whole pipelines. They're a preview: releases ship with plugins switched off, and an administrator turns them on with `SP_PLUGINS_ENABLED` (see [Feature toggles](../environment-variables.md#feature-toggles)). The SDK for writing them is published to npm as a preview (`@serene-pub/sdk` and its companion packages, version `0.6.0-pr-1`, on the `next` tag), with guides under **SDK guides** in Help. It may still change before its first stable release in 0.7.

## Upgrading from 0.5.3

You don't export or import anything. 0.6 opens your 0.5.3 data and converts it the first time it starts.

1. Close 0.5.3.
2. Download 0.6 and extract it to a **new folder** (see [Install](../install.md)). Your data isn't kept in the app's folder, so 0.6 finds it where 0.5.3 left it. If you moved your data with `SERENE_PUB_DATA_DIR`, start 0.6 with the same setting.
3. Start 0.6 and wait. It takes a backup, then converts everything before the first page opens. A big database can take a few minutes.
4. Sign in as usual. Administrators get a notification, *Your 0.5.3 data was upgraded to 0.6*; **Open History** lists every note the upgrade left.

**What comes across:** accounts and their settings, characters with their avatars and galleries, personas, every chat (as a Chat session, with swipes, reasoning, edits and hidden messages), lorebooks with their scenes and relationship graph, connections and API keys, sampling configs, your embedding setup, the prompts you wrote, themes and tags. Sessions that used 0.5.3's built-in reply prompt keep it; new sessions start on 0.6's default.

**Connections are combined.** A 0.5.3 connection was one model; a 0.6 connection is one service with many models. Connections to the same service (same kind, address and API key) become one connection listing every model they used, named the way 0.6 names a new connection to that service: twenty Ollama connections to `localhost:11434` become one **Ollama** connection, and a second OpenRouter key becomes **OpenRouter 2**. A note lists what was combined or renamed, with your 0.5.3 names.

**Where things moved:**

- Chats are in the **Sessions** view.
- Personas are characters marked as personas, in the **Characters** view.
- Which model replies is set once in **Admin › Models › Defaults** (or per pipeline), not per chat.
- Prompts belong to pipelines, in **Admin › Pipelines**, and context configs are replaced by **context templates**.

**What doesn't come across** (it stays in the backup, and the upgrade notes list it): a chat's own connection, everything in a prompt config except its text, context configs you wrote, character card assets, persona list order, the summarization and easy persona creation switches, and stored embeddings made by a different model than your current one (they're made again; on a paid service that first re-index is billed once).

To go back to 0.5.3, restore the backup taken before the upgrade. Anything you did in 0.6 stays only in the newer database. The full story, including what each note means, is in [Upgrading from 0.5](../upgrading-from-0.5.md).

## Known issues and limits

- **A preview never updates itself.** Pre-release builds have no **Admin › Updates** and never check for new versions. To move to the next preview or to 0.6.0, quit Serene Pub, download the new build and extract it over this one's folder. Your data is untouched, and Serene Pub backs it up before a new version changes it.
- **Windows and macOS warn about an unknown app.** The builds aren't signed. On Windows, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. On macOS, open **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. You only do this once; see [Install](../install.md).
- **Image generation is basic in this preview.** Chat's **Image** action draws a picture with an AUTOMATIC1111-compatible program or the KoboldCPP run by Serene Pub. Generating avatars or sprite sets, ComfyUI and LoRAs aren't in this preview. KoboldCPP holds one model at a time, so a picture unloads the chat model, and with large models reloading can take minutes.
- **Hybrid models on KoboldCPP** (Qwen 3.5, 3.6 and 3.8, and others KoboldCPP calls *RNN or Hybrid*) reuse their cache only when a prompt starts exactly like the last one. Lore chosen for each turn sits near the top of the prompt, so some replies re-read more than you'd expect. See [Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting.md).
- **After upgrading a big 0.5.3 database**, Serene Pub indexes your lore and messages in the background for a while. Replies come first, but search by meaning finds less until it finishes.
- **Plugins are switched off** in releases, and the SDK is a preview whose names may change before 0.7.
- **Lorebook export is paused** while the format settles. Importing still works (SillyTavern World Info and 0.5 lorebooks), and a character card can still carry a book.
- **Not here yet:** impersonate (the model writing your persona's lines), voice (text-to-speech and speech input), and more than one lorebook per session. Some parts of stats aren't built yet either; see [What isn't built yet](../stats-and-states.md#what-isnt-built-yet).
- **Visual polish:** message styles and widgets haven't all had a full visual pass. Small layout glitches are possible, especially in narrow windows and custom styles.
- **Platform limits:** Intel Macs can't run the built-in local embedding models (use an online one). Linux on ARM is Docker only. The Android app can't run KoboldCPP or Ollama for you and has no local embedding models, though it connects to ones running elsewhere. The Docker image for this preview is tagged `0.6.0-pr-1` only; `latest` stays on the newest stable release.
- Service presets marked *Experimental* in the **New connection** dialog have had less testing.

## Reporting a bug

Thank you for trying the preview. When something goes wrong:

1. Check [Troubleshooting](../troubleshooting.md) first: many problems have a one-line fix.
2. If you're an administrator, copy a support report from **Admin › Diagnostics › Copy report**. It has the versions, settings and recent errors a fix usually needs, with secrets and names removed.
3. [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/doolijb/serene-pub/issues) with what you did, what you expected and what happened instead, plus the support report. Mention that you're on **0.6.0-pr-1**, and say if you upgraded from 0.5.3.

Questions and quick help are welcome on [Discord](https://discord.gg/3kUx3MDcSa) too.
