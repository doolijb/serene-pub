# Serene Pub 0.6.0-pr-7 release notes

A preview of 0.6, released in October 2026. It is about room to play and room to extend: a full-screen session, new ways to start one, a story clock that keeps better time, and plugins that carry their own files, strings, rules and actions. Everything in [0.6.0-pr-1](./0.6.0-pr-1.md) still applies; read those notes first if you're coming from 0.5.3.

> **This is a preview build.** Your data comes across by itself, and a backup is taken before anything changes.

## New

- **Full screen.** Press **Ctrl Shift F** (**⌘ Shift F** on a Mac), or **Full screen** in the session's header (in its **⋯** on a phone), and everything but the story steps away. **Esc** or **Exit full screen** in the corner brings it back. The corner control fades to a thin edge after two seconds and returns when you point at it, touch it or tab to it. It is remembered for each session. See [Getting around](../getting-around.md#full-screen).
- **A session type can open in full screen.** A session type with its own session view may open a session in full screen the first time, with a one-line notice (*Press Esc to leave full screen.*, or *Tap the corner to leave full screen.* on a touch screen). After that, your own choice wins.
- **Four ways to start a session.** **How it starts** on the start form offers **New session**, **New world** (copies the lorebook first), **Branch** (picks up another session's history) and **Next chapter**, which follows a finished session: its rules' carried-over stats and your seat come with it, it begins with *Chapter begins.*, and its messages stay behind. A session type may suggest one. See [Sessions](../sessions.md#how-it-starts).
- **Update rules.** When a session's rules have a newer version, its owner can choose **Update rules** in the session's settings. The confirm lists what changes and ends *Back undoes this.* A session type can also update rules itself when a session opens, and says so in one line. Sessions keep playing the version they started on until then.
- **Story clock moves until an hour.** A step can move the clock until a time instead of by an amount, and moves nothing when it is already there.
- **Hourly routines.** A rule can run each hour the clock passes, or only while awake. Until a story has a clock, hourly routines wait and the session notes it once.
- **Parts of the day and seasons.** A world's calendar can name its day periods and seasons. **Part of the day** and **Season** are read from the story clock, never stored.
- **Flag attributes, world scope and owner.** Rules can declare a yes-or-no **flag**, say **Held by** whom (the player, each character, the world or each place) and whether it is kept **For the session** or **Across sessions**, on the lorebook. See [Stats and states](../stats-and-states.md#who-holds-an-attribute-and-for-how-long).
- **Schedules and the cast by hour.** A character can have a weekly **schedule** of where they are by hour, and the cast reads it for the story clock's hour. A daily **weather** routine picks the day's weather without a model, and a household example shows a character reaching out to the player.
- **A character to start with.** The setup wizard's **Pick a character** offers **Nell Haverly** first, marked **Comes with Serene Pub**: she keeps the Ninth Mile, a coaching inn where three roads meet. She's added to your characters only when you press **Pick**. See [Your first session](../getting-started.md#4-pick-a-character).
- **A second starter lorebook, The Ninth Mile.** Nell's inn, listed first: four entries about the inn and its roads, a lead to start from, and the Freeform rules, with no dice. See [Starter lorebooks](../lorebooks.md#starter-lorebooks).
- **Free models on OpenRouter, listed first.** On a key with no credit, the connection lists OpenRouter's free models first, and its status card and the setup wizard say so: about 50 replies a day. See [Free models](../connect-a-model.md#free-models).
- **KoboldCPP set up for your computer.** Serene Pub looks at the graphics card in the computer it runs on, picks the KoboldCPP build that suits it and proposes a memory tier, each with the reason in one line. Your own choice wins. See [Connect a model](../connect-a-model.md#if-you-chose-local-koboldcpp-recommended).
- **Notices from plugins.** A plugin's notices appear in the app's own toaster. A sticky one waits in **Activity**.
- **Channel audiences.** A channel can be seen only by its participants: a thread between two characters stays out of everyone else's view.
- **Saved state on a lorebook.** A plugin or session type can keep free-form saved state on a lorebook, shared by every session that reads it. See [The lorebook annex](../lorebooks.md#the-lorebook-annex).
- **Plugin packages live on disk, with limits.** A package unpacks under your data folder. `SP_PLUGIN_PACKAGE_MAX_MB` (512) and `SP_PLUGIN_FILES_MAX` (10000) bound it, and a refusal names the variable. See [Plugins](../plugins.md).
- **Review a package before it installs.** Choosing a zip in **Admin › Plugins** sends nothing yet: the page shows the plugin's name and version, **Permissions it asks for** and **What it can't do**. An installed plugin's page keeps **What it can't do** in step with what you approve. See [What a plugin can't do](../plugins.md#what-a-plugin-cant-do).
- **A package can install from a folder**, for a machine you reach only by a terminal: stop Serene Pub and run `npm run plugin:install -- <folder>`. See [Hosting](../hosting.md#install-a-package-from-disk).
- **Plugin pictures, sound and data are served from disk**, at an address with the plugin's version in it, so each file is kept for good and an upgrade never shows an old one.
- **Plugin languages.** A package can carry `lang/<locale>.json` string catalogs. A plugin's words read in your language from its own catalog first, then the app's automatic translations, then English.
- **A plugin's own storage and shared files.** A plugin may list files to share with its own pages, readable at `/plugin-data/…` and nothing else.
- **Plugin actions.** A plugin's page or frame can call a named piece of work the package offers, and a frame gets the result back. **File pick** and **file save** go through the app and an action; the file's bytes never reach the frame.
- **Autoplay needs permission.** A plugin's pages play sound by themselves only when the package asked for it and you accepted.
- **A frame can save more.** A frame's saved view state may be up to 256 KB.
- **Rules in a package.** Installing a package publishes the rules it brings; uninstalling retires them.

## Changed

- **Plugin storage is on by default.** Before, a plugin's own storage was silently off unless `SERENE_PUB_DATA_DIR` was set. It is now on for every pub, 64 MB each unless the package asks for another size.
- **The support report** shows what each plugin takes on disk, under **Plugin disk use**.
- **Plugins are on by default.** They run on every pub unless the person who runs it sets `SP_PLUGINS_ENABLED` to `0` (or `false`, `no`, `off`) and restarts. There's no switch in the app yet; **Admin › Plugins** says when the variable decides. See [Feature toggles](../environment-variables.md#feature-toggles).
- **No 70 MB upload limit.** The browser uploads a package of any size up to `SP_PLUGIN_PACKAGE_MAX_MB`, and `BODY_SIZE_LIMIT` needs no raising.

## Removed

- **Stage only** and its shortcut are gone. **Full screen** replaces it.

## Not in 0.6

People ask for these, and they're left out of 0.6 on purpose, not forgotten:

- **Modifiers**, such as being poisoned, a resistance or a proficiency, as a general way for rules to change a character's numbers while an effect lasts.
- **An iOS app.** On an iPhone or iPad, open a pub that runs on a computer in the browser instead (see [Install Serene Pub](../install.md#everyday-use)).
- **Lorebook export, and a lorebook format of Serene Pub's own.** **Export** stays paused while the format settles. A [character card](../characters.md) can still carry a book's main line.
- **The `.serene` package format**, one file Serene Pub reads without unpacking. It comes in 0.7; until then, plugins come as `.zip` packages.

## For plugin authors

The SDK packages are at `0.6.0-pr-7` on npm (under the `next` tag). New in the SDK: `assets` (a folder served read-only), `pluginActions`, `rulesets` and `lang/<locale>.json` in a package; `permissions.storage.serve` and `permissions.media.autoplay`; the storage default of 64 MB, in the test harness too; the frame's `init.assetsBase` and `init.fullscreen`, `invoke` with a `requestId` answered by a `response`, and the requests `file-pick`, `file-save`, `notify` and `session-fullscreen`; `SessionShape.fullscreen` and `SessionShape.creationMode`; `scope: 'lorebook'` on annex fields and the `lorebook-annex` query and outlets; `nextChapter` on annex fields and attributes; the channel audience `{ participants }`; the flag attribute kind, `scope` and `owner` on attributes (`carriedBy` still reads), the `schedule` field, the day-period and season slots, and the `advance-story-clock` step's `to`. Guides: *Frames*, *Storage*, *Rulesets*, *Channels* and a new *Time* guide.

`serene-pub check` now refuses everything `serene-pub build` refuses, and still writes nothing: a component that doesn't build (`E_COMPONENT_BUILD`) or a sandbox bundle that doesn't (`E_SANDBOX_BUNDLE`) fails both the same way. Reinstalling a plugin replaces its own node definitions instead of refusing them. A new guide, [What the SDK promises before 1.0](../sdk/guides/stability.md), says what you can rely on and what may still change.

## Downloads

The same downloads as before: Windows, macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), Linux, Android, and the Docker image. See [Install Serene Pub](../install.md). How the Windows download is signed is in the [Code signing policy](../code-signing-policy.md).

## Help test what models can do

A connection's **What this connection can do** marks anything nobody has confirmed with a real service as **Assumed**. If you can, try a few items from the [0.6 test checklist](https://github.com/doolijb/serene-pub/blob/v0.6.0-pr-7/TESTING-CHECKLIST-0.6.0.md) with your own model, and send each result with the [Capability report](https://github.com/doolijb/serene-pub/issues/new?template=capability-report.yml) form on GitHub. Results for **Assumed** items help most, whether they work or not.

## Reporting a problem

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/doolijb/serene-pub/issues) with what you did, what you expected, and what happened. An administrator can copy a support report to paste in, from **Admin › Diagnostics**.
