## Landing rule (`src/lib/server/state/worldScope.ts` `worldOwnerOf`)
- **A player's seat with a persona card** is treated as that persona (`session_cast`); a seat with no card keeps the refusal word for word (_A player's seat holds nothing that lives on the world._).
