## The param
★ `speakerInCast`, label ★ *Only scenes the speaker was in*: boolean, default `true`, last field of `params` on `earlier-scenes@1` and `history-entries@1` (history gets its own slot builder, `historySlots`; world-lore and character-lore byte-identical). The speaker is resolved as `knownToSpeaker` resolves it; a narrator turn, or a card in no seat, reads everything.
- **earlier-scenes:** only scenes whose stored cast lists the speaker as a participant; an open scene is kept; a scene whose cast was never worked out (`cast_resolved_at` NULL) is kept.
- **history-entries**, for days with this session's scenes compiled in: in every scene → the whole day (including any other session's scenes, not judged ★); missed some → the lane's own copy of the day is replaced by the summaries of the scenes it was in, in story order, under the day's date, never longer than the day's own token count (oldest summaries dropped first ★); in none, or nothing usable fits → the day goes on `exclusions` (so does a partly missed day the lane did not itself return). A hand-written day is untouched.
- When `firsthandFor` is also wired, firsthand runs first. `false` restores today's behaviour. `session-history@1` untouched.
