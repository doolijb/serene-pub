## New names (🚧) — §25 row text
- **appletCard** — `ExtensionDecl.appletCard`, the compiled manifest, the `sessions:genres` row member, the mount fields. The applet's card: the art and line a package declares for its applet (`poster`, `logo`, `blurb`). On the row it is the card as served, `{ title, poster?, logo?, blurb? }`, present exactly when the launcher mounts; replaces `appletTitle` (M2c 🚧, removed). Purpose: one source for the Apps view, Home and the launcher. Owns display only; does not own the grant (`permissions.applet`) or the genre flag (`shape.applet`) — hence the qualified name (R3).
- **poster** — the applet card's picture; package-relative under `assets.dir`, served at `/plugin-assets/<id>/<version>/<path>`; shown 16:9 and cropped; 1280×720 recommended; no ratio check. Not a session cover, a lorebook cover or an avatar.
- **logo** — the card's optional small square mark over the poster's lower-left; same path rule. Not a service (vendor) logo.
- **blurb** — the card's one short descriptive line, ≤ 160 chars after `i18nText`; same sense as the UI-copy "blurb" (§5.2).
- `appletCardFindings` — SDK `@experimental`, the one card rule, run by `serene-pub check` and at install.
- `E_APPLET_CARD_OUTSIDE_ASSETS` · `E_APPLET_CARD_MISSING` · `E_APPLET_CARD_BLURB_LONG` · `E_APPLET_CARD_WITHOUT_APPLET` — §16's `E_APPLET_ART_*`/`E_APPLET_BLURB_LONG`, named under the lane's §24 call.
- `W_SETTING_UNDESCRIBED` — ★E, the packager's P5 warning (see above). Warning only.
