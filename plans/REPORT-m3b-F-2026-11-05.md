## Decisions taken (named)

## ★ m3b-F-1 — provided rows' group (built as recommended)
**Recommend: their own group, _Ready-made_, above _Shared with you_.** A provided book is not a person's share (it is attachable, pub-wide, nobody's act), and the Layouts tab already settled the same mislabel with this word (owner-ruled 2026-10-14: "A pub genre's layout, mislabelled _Shared with you_ …"). **Trade-off:** a member's shelf gains a third group, and the word is narrower here than in Layouts — here it holds only core's and plugins' books (core primer rows move into it too, out of _Shared with you_), never an admin's own starter lorebook, which Layouts' _Ready-made_ includes as _This pub_. The admin who holds these rows still sees them among their own books (unchanged). Alternative: keep them under _Shared with you_ with only the line changed — no new label, but the heading keeps contradicting the row's _Comes with …_.
