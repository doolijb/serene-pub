## The rule, and where it lives
- **The cast read** (`pipelines/runtime/host.read.sessionCast.ts`, the `storyTime` block): when the story has no present, it adds `timeline: false` to `storyTime` (the word with the meaning `SessionSettingsV1.timeline` gives it). A session that keeps a timeline gets no key and no extra statement.
- **Which read:** `sessionTimelineOf`, the read `loreWriteDateOf`'s `timeless` answer rests on (`sessionReadingOf`'s `timelineOff` = `!timelineOf(row)`). Calling `loreWriteDateOf` itself cost +70 to +100 statements a turn on undated sessions; this costs +11 to +13.
- **Assemble** (`pipelines/prompt/assemble.ts`): one predicate, `keepsNoTimeline`, in the three helpers every part goes through: `currentDateOf` returns `undefined` (checked before the fallback to the newest history entry), `datedOf` returns null date and delta, `calendarOf` returns `null`.
