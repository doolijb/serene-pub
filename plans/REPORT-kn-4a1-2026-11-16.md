## The door (🚧 names; `server/sockets/entryKnowledge.ts`)

- `entryKnowledge:list` `{lorebookId, entryId?, castMemberId?, branchId, moment}`: always the book's
  `markedEntryIds`; with `entryId` a row per cast member; with `castMemberId` every marked entry plus every entry
  the member is tied to; with both, one pair. Row: `{castMemberId, name, entryId, known, winner{tieId, kind,
  relationshipType, hand, date}, hand, placeEntry}`, judged by `winningTies` (extracted from
  `judgeEntryKnowledge`, behaviour unchanged) on this book's own ties at the workspace's line and moment.
- `entryKnowledge:setCommonKnowledge` `{lorebookId, entryIds[], notCommonKnowledge}`: one outer transaction, all
  or none, one reply.
- `entryKnowledge:setTie` `{lorebookId, castMemberId, entryId, hand: "knows" | "kept_from" | null, branchId,
  moment}`: ends the member's live hand ties, then writes the new one, one transaction.
- Gate: `book.userId === userId` only (an administrator who is not the owner gets nothing; a session member who
  is not the notes book's owner gets nothing). Scoped by `lorebookId`, gated, primer books refused. One client
  listener `BookKnowledge` (provided by `EntryWorkspace`); it never toasts.
- **Hand tie** = a `knows` / `kept_from` tie with `history_entry_id` and `branch_id` both NULL.

## Precedence (unchanged; D39)

Later date, then nearer line, then *kept from*. Hand ties are undated: any dated tie beats them; an undated tie
on the fork being read is nearer than a hand tie on main; between undated ties at the same nearness *kept from*
wins. The screen says when the switch loses.
