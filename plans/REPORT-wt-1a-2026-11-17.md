## The rule as built

`sceneCastSpeakers` (`src/lib/server/scenes/liveScenes.ts:642`, 🚧): every cast member that **sent** at least one line in the scene's range, kept by its sender card. Place rule: for each line a member sent, its own `core:slot/location@1` as of that line (`valueOf({ asOfMessageId })`, no world fallback) against the scene's place at that line (`scenePlaceKeyOf`, `worldStateReaderWith`; compared with `sameName`); a line passes when the genre declares no place key, the member has no own place, the scene has no place, or one of the member's places names the scene's place. ★ A member is listed when **at least one** of its lines passes. Benching plays no part. **Not listed:** the roster, and names found in lines (speech tags, people spoken to, gazetteer hits).

## SC2 (`sc-2a`, 2026-11-17) — a speech tag in a narrator line counts as having spoken
