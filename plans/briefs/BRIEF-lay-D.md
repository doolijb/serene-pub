## Goal
The layout editor edits panels v2: three zone stacks, no rail strip / Crown / primary, Fit · Fill + **Priority** (High · Normal · Low) per panel, and an inline **inspector** that shows the chosen tab's settings, or the layout's own **Backing** · **Opacity** · **Widget gap** when nothing is chosen. No settings window anywhere.
