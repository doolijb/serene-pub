## What was built (verify each point in code before you write it)
1. **No pin, no rail of icons, no fly-outs, no primary/main panel, no strips above/below, no tucked sides, no Session panels button or sheet, no Alt+[ / Alt+] for session panels** (those keys are the app's again). Every panel is always open. The middle is a plain stack like the sides; the one rule kept: the middle is never empty (editor refusal *Put something in the middle first.* — check the exact string in arrangeOps.ts `MIDDLE_EMPTY`).
2. **Three widgets for the conversation**: **Messages** (the log, channel tabs, the selection bar at its foot), **Turn line** (what the last run did and who is next; one setting **Who is due next**), **Composer** (the field you write in; settings **Composer**, **Composer on the other tabs**, **Show the Actions label**). Messages keeps Line width, Presentation, Message order, Channel group, Reading group (incl. 🚧 When empty, Story time format). Messages LOST: Composer, Composer on the other tabs, Composer position, Show messages, Show composer, Who is due next, Show the Actions label, and **Card** (Auto/On/Off).
3. **Which Messages a Composer writes to** (`conversationTarget.ts`): the nearest panel above it in the same zone whose shown tab is a Messages copy; else the nearest Messages panel in its zone; else the layout's first Messages. If that panel is showing another tab, the Composer keeps the last channel and says *Writing to ‹channel›*. The Turn line follows the same target. Line width and presentation follow the target. Composer and Turn line wear their target Messages' **style** unless you pinned one of their own. A zone with Messages and no Composer is a reading pane (order is panel order: put the Composer above the Messages panel to write at the top).
4. **Heights** (`zoneHeights.ts`): each panel is **Fit** or **Fill** (plus the divider's share in twelfths between two panels that aren't Fit — check ArrangeCanvas comment ~:38 for where the divider appears now), and has a **Priority** High · Normal · Low (default from the widget: Composer High, Turn line Normal, Messages Low, others Normal; the editor shows the effective value). When a zone runs short of height, Low gives way first, then Normal; High keeps its height longest. Messages never goes below 160px; a Fit panel never below its content or 120px, whichever is less; a panel given less than it wants scrolls inside itself. Read `allocateZone` for the exact order before writing.
5. **Zone switch** (`sessionZones.svelte.ts`, `ZoneSwitch.svelte`, `SessionLayoutRender.svelte:294`): a **Left · Middle · Right** switch shows one zone at a time. Always on below 1024px (lg) — on a phone it is its own row under the header; on a desktop it sits in the header and turns on when the sides don't fit beside a readable middle (e.g. a sidebar view opened beside the session). Only zones that hold a panel are offered, and it draws only with two or more. It starts on the zone holding the layout's first Messages (the primary log), else Middle (`openingZone` in placementRules.ts — check the exact rule). Its accessible name is "Zone shown".
6. **Full screen** (`placementRules.ts` `fullscreenActive`): shows the WHOLE opening zone (its Messages, Turn line, Composer and anything else stacked there) and no zone switch; Esc/again brings everything back. Check whether the other shell parts (rail, bars, sidebar) still step aside as getting-around.md says.
7. **Settings live only in the layout editor** (R4): no gear and no pin anywhere in the live session (PanelTabs.svelte comment :8). On the **Arrange** tab, choosing a tab on a card (or its gear, "‹Widget› settings") opens it in the **inspector** pane beside the canvas: header "‹Widget›" with "‹kind› · ‹Zone›, ‹nth› panel" under it, then its settings, then its Style section. With nothing chosen the pane is **Layout settings**: **Backing** (*Card* · *Glass* · *None*; "What every panel sits on. How a card and glass look comes from your theme."), **Opacity** (0–100%; "Lower lets your background show through."), **Widget gap** (*Tight* · *Comfortable* · *Spacious* = 4/12/24px; "The space between panels and around the zones."), and **For style authors** (the four `--sp-widget-*` names). Defaults: Card, 100%, Comfortable. Look edits are covered by Undo/Cancel; widget settings still apply as you change them (check, and say it plainly). The Layouts tab's style overlay **Settings** selects that tab in Arrange (WidgetStyleOverlay / `askSettingsInArrange`).
8. **Every widget's own common settings** are now **Title** and **Lane** only (`src/lib/shared/widgets/settings.ts` `CORE_SETTING_KEYS`); the per-widget **Card** is gone. The backing is drawn on the **panel** (the tab strip sits on the card). A widget opened over the session for a moment (a pop-over) still has its own card — check `hostCard.ts`.
9. **For style authors** (`panelBacking.ts`): the panel box carries `--sp-widget-margin` (0), `--sp-widget-background`, `--sp-widget-radius`, `--sp-widget-border` from the layout's backing and opacity. The shown tab's style may set any of the four (in its CSS or its variables; variables win, last wins) to change its panel's box; a value with `url(`, a backslash, `;`, `{`, `}`, `<`, or one the style filter would change is ignored. *Dreamlit Cameo* sets the background transparent and no border. `--sp-backing` is gone. **Messages has five styles** — re-check that list.
