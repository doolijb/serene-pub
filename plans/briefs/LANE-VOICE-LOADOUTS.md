## A. Voicebank entries bind to a LOADOUT
1. Generated migration: `voicebank_voices.loadout_id` (int, FK loadouts on delete set null → entry becomes "unassigned", skipped at resolution with a note), back-filled to the `text->audio` default loadout (else the `core:loadout/tts@1` seed row) for existing rows. Keep `connection_model_id` as a derived mirror written on save and on a loadout change (many readers use it: facts, pickable, presence) — or replace it everywhere if that is cleaner; say which you did.
2. Entry form: a **Loadout** picker listing loadouts whose `transform` is `text->audio` (name + its pair; unfilled loadouts shown greyed "no model yet"); voices and `facts` come from the chosen loadout's model; `assertOnPair` goes (A7 retired); `ensureVoicebankDefault` seeds on the default speech loadout. `voicebank:pickable`/list/rows carry `loadoutId` + loadout name.
3. Speak time: the resolver returns the entry's loadout (`loadoutId`, pair); `dispatchSpeech` resolves the **target per group** (adapter + facts + controls + limit per group; keep one in-flight per group as today); the preview takes the entry's loadout. Mixed models in one line: `joinAudio` gains a PCM resampler to one rate (the first part's, or 24 kHz) for WAV parts that differ only in rate/channels — linear interpolation is fine; mixed formats still fall back to first-only with the note. Managed runtimes: switching models between groups is allowed (audio.cpp lazy-loads; KoboldCPP reloads) — the receipt notes the switch.
4. Who may pick a loadout: admins (members never touch loadouts).

6. The card's own voice keeps customisation: pick a voicebank **Voice** (base) and layer description/language/speed/seed/dials over it (today's timbre), through the shared fields.
