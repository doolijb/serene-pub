For a card-less version (`carded: false`) return `{}` (today a card-less member's columns are simply its own).
  - `ownName`: the version's `name` when `carded` and `name` is not followed, else null. `ownNickname`: when `carded` and `nickname` not followed, the version's nickname, with a null nickname written as `""` (★L-3b: own `""` = no nickname in this book; check `memberProfileOf`'s nickname rule and `ownNameValue` so the two round-trip), else null.
