## What was built (the facts to write; nothing beyond them)

- The session's owner moves a saved seat between a person's **Personas** group (a player seat) and **Characters** (the seats the story plays), with **Played by** in **Edit session › Participants**:
  - On a persona that has a card or a cast member (the owner's own, or **any guest's own persona**): **No one** moves it to Characters.
  - On a character holding the owner's own card or a cast member with no card: **You as a persona** or **‹Name› as a persona** (each guest who plays) moves it to that person's Personas group. On a character holding a guest's own card: only **‹Guest› as a persona**, back to that guest. A guest's card never goes to another person.
  - Picking a move shows one sentence in the row (*‹Name› moves to the characters the story plays when you save. Its earlier lines stay as they are.* / *‹Name› moves to ‹Person›'s personas when you save. …*), with **Move** and **Cancel**; after **Move** the row says *Moves on Save*; nothing changes until **Save**.
  - Not offered: a seat with a name but no card or cast member; a card or cast member that already has a seat of the other kind. Refused in words: while it is that seat's turn; while a reply is being written; when the genre has no seats of that kind. Only the owner moves seats; guests see nothing new.
- After a move: one seat of the new kind for the same character or cast member; the old seat is gone (a seat moved back is the same seat again). Values, sheet, bench state and the seat's place in the turn order follow it (the turn order recomputes).
- **Earlier lines stay exactly as they were** (MV2): a person's typed lines stay that person's (shown under the character, still theirs to edit; the owner gains nothing); the story's lines stay the story's (the new person cannot edit them as their own; whoever could edit them before still can); the model reads them as written. After a persona moves to Characters, its person no longer writes as it in this session; after a character moves to a person, that person writes as it and takes its turns.
- An unanswered question (a form) put to a seat that moved: to a persona that moved to Characters, the owner answers it; to a character that moved to a person, that person answers it.
- The existing in-place handover (**No one** / a guest on a character; **You** / a guest on a persona) is unchanged and is not a move.
