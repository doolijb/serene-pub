### Benched seats in one paragraph

A seat is **in play** when it is live and not benched (`seatInPlay` / `inPlay`). A benched seat leaves the turns and the characters block, and its lore reads as background. `setSeatBench` (`src/lib/server/sessions/seats.ts`) is the **only writer** of `is_active`. Core never benches on its own. A genre opts in with `writes.seats` and uses `core:outlet/set-seat-bench@1`. That outlet is tied to the turn through the `seatBenches` core annex key: Back, regenerate and delete undo it, and a person's later move wins. Removing a seat benches it first; only an unused benched seat is soft-removed. Upgrade 0152 and the 0.5.3 attic bench removed seats that have lines. Dungeon Crawler companions can die or be revived (`fallenCompanions` / `revivedCompanions`). A guest whose only seat is benched sees "You're on the bench — the owner can bring you back."
