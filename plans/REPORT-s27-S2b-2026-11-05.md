### ★pT-2: scene pictures
`pipelines/runtime/illustrateScene.ts` `likenessImagesFor` picks, per seat, in stage order:
1. the **active** card's likeness image;
2. else the member's own face (own means `cardless` or `!followsCard.includes("face")`);
3. else the active card's avatar.

Player seats read their member too. A card-less player seat with a member offers that member's own face, and a seat with no member reads its card as before. The paragraph about scene references in `docs/sessions.md` (~187) is updated to match.

### ★client-2: search matches the shown name
`sockets/jump.ts` `castMatches` has two more `EXISTS` clauses, one each over `session_characters` and `session_personas`. Each joins `lorebook_bindings` by the seat's `cast_member_id` and matches `own_name ILIKE` or `own_nickname ILIKE`. The existing card-name matches stay.

**For lane s27-S2c:**
- **Search rule:** a session matches when a seat's card name matches (as before) or a seat's cast member's own name or own nickname matches, for character and player seats. On the client, that is matching the payload's `castNames` values, which now include persona seats, keyed by the seat's card id.
- **Payload maps:** the shape is unchanged. `castNames`, `castFaces` and `castSpriteSets` now carry persona seats' card ids too. A card held by both a character seat and a player seat keys the character seat's entry. `castNames` carries the member's shown name only, so the client still lets a player seat's seat name win.

## Open ★ (for the owner)
- **★S2b-1:** when the member has no active card on the reading (a dated amendment unlinks it), ownership, "can act" and fire-turn fall back to the **seat's card** owner. The same fallback is in `ownsSeatedCard`, the fireTurn `ownsIt`, `handEdits`, `sockets/state.ts`, `seatName` and `seatPortrait`. The alternative is that nobody but the session owner may act. My recommendation is to keep the fallback, so a guest is not locked out of their own seat. It is marked ★ in the §25 row.
