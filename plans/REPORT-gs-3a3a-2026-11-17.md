## Scope review fix (gs-3a3a-fix, 2026-11-17)

The person of a carded player seat is `coalesce(session_personas.user_id, characters.user_id)` everywhere.

1. Portrayals: `presenceOwner` (the coalesce), the seat reference (`seat.actingUserId ?? card?.userId`), `ownPresence` (the `personaOf` test). Earshot, `audienceHiddenSlugsFor`, `dueUserOf` and the press voicing follow without edits. Tests: `sessions.ownerCardForGuest.int` 12-14.
2. `rotationPersonas`: `seatUserId ?? persona.userId` (test 15).
3. `whoFor`: `who.owner` is the owner's persona through `personaOf`; with none, the first persona seated that names nobody (test 16).
4. `maySpeakAsCard`: a removed seat or a person no longer a member falls back to the card's owner; `removeGuest` hands back player seats on the owner's card (`handOverSeat`); `seatPlayer`'s upsert writes `input.userId` on a revived row, and the persona-list add passes the adder as `userId` when they own the card. Tests 17-19 (test 10 now expects the owner as the stored person).
5. `playerSeatsFor`: inside the transaction the row is read before the write; a live row with a person refuses with `alreadySeated`, and the handover's `fromUserId` is that pre-read value. Test 20 (hook between check and write).
6. Client form-answer cast: `persona.userId` is the seat's person. The SDK line `formAnswer.ts:110` reads `presence.persona?.userId`, so the app-side change alone makes a form addressed to the guest's character reach the guest (traced: the cast projection is the only source of that field). The SDK needs no edit for this; its `player-seat` case already reads `seat.userId ?? seat.persona.userId`.
7. Model proposals follow HM1, no code change; pinned in `handEdits.cardOwner.int` (guest refused, owner accepts; a card-less seat stays the guest's).
