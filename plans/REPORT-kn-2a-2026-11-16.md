## What the param drops, per read

`knownToSpeaker` (boolean, default `true`, 🚧) is the last field of each read's `params` in
`contracts/src/queries.ts`. The drop happens at the source, the host's `lorebook_entries` read
(`host.read.lorebookEntries.ts` ~812–862); each read passes `knownToSpeaker: params.knownToSpeaker !== false`
and logs a receipt note.

- world-lore, character-lore, history-entries (`loreFor`): unknown entries never enter the scan; pinned entries too.
- lorebook-triggers: an unknown entry neither triggers nor is scanned in recursion.
- lorebook-entries: not listed; no row's `withLinks` names it as a far end.
- vector-search, entity-search: a hit whose entry was dropped is withheld.
- entity-link: with the param on it also reads when `speaker` is unwired (the scope's speaker).
- relationship-search (the hop): `chosen` and `visible` come from the filtered read; passes `currentCastMemberId`.
- entries-named: `entriesNamedIn(…, { leaveOut, onLeftOut })` returns neither text nor name of an unknown entry.
- No param on `mention-spans` (message spans only) or `relationships-known` / `-perspectives` (cast-to-cast only).

## Speaker

The read's wired `speaker`; else the scope's subject through the host's resolution (`seated-member:<id>` for a
card-less seat, `character:<card>` otherwise); no subject → reads everything. A card in no seat (Story's side
character) reads everything, as before. Judgement loaded once per run and speaker in the host's per-run
`lorebookReads` map (`knowledge:<session>:<ref>`), freed with the host.

## Standing-place exception (new)

Runs only when something is left out and a left-out entry is of type `core:entry/location`; reads
`core:slot/location@1` on the speaker's cast owner (`castOwnerOf`), falling back to the session's world value;
an entry reference or a `sameName` match keeps that place, even against a *kept from* tie.
