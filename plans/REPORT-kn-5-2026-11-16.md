## A. The precedence as built (comment at `judgeEntryKnowledge`)
1. The place the speaker stands in counts as known, whatever the ties say.
2. Of the ties that read for a pair, a **hand tie** beats every other tie: play's ties, ordinary relationships, and dated or branch `knows` / `kept_from`. A hand tie is `knows` / `kept_from` with no history entry and no branch (one predicate, `isHandTie`). Of two hand ties the nearer wins (the session's notes, then the line's steps), then the newest id.
3. With no hand tie, unchanged: later date, then nearer line, then *kept from*.
4. The winning tie decides: *kept from* leaves the entry out; any other tie means known.
5. No tie reads: a marked entry is not known, an unmarked one is common knowledge.

Every reader goes through the judge (the run's filter, core tools, **Known by**, **Knows**). The row's `hand` is the kind of the winning hand tie; a new row field ★ `overruled` holds the tie that would win without the hand tie.
