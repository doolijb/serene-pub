## 2. The typed fact

🚧 `SessionSettingsV1.timeline: boolean` (required), on the settings document every run gets as
`$.input.session`, in the `session-settings@1` re-read and a script's `session` extra; filled once per run in
`sessions/settings.ts` `resolveSessionSettings` from `sessionTimelineOf`. Docs promise: it holds for the
session's life unless the lorebook's owner flips the switch; `false`: story time does not move and nothing dated
is written; `true`: no date is promised yet (`current-date` is absent while the line has no clock and no dated
history entry).

## 3. The timeline switch and lines with no story date (no data changed)

Switching on (`sockets/lorebooks.ts` 1059–1155) writes the column and copies it to the lorebook's notes; undated
history entries stay undated (read at every date, never count toward the present, listed under Loose ends ›
Undated history). `timelinePass.ts` is a one-time boot pass that only switches lorebooks off. A timeline line
with no story date arises from: a new blank lorebook or a session's own notes (no clock, `ownBook.ts:233`);
Story and the Lair setting no clock at create (Dungeon Crawler does); a timeless copy switched back on;
switching on a lorebook whose history entries are all legacy undated; an import or 0.5 upgrade with no history.
Every new lorebook starts in this state.
