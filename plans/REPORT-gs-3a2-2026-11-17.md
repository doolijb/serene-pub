## Scope review fix (gs-3a2-fix)

1. **Guarded handover write.** `handOverSeat` (`sessions/seats.ts`) now matches the seat's current person (`fromUserId`, null = no one) and, for a guest's take, `requireActive`. `writeSeatHandovers` (`sessions/guestSeats.ts`) runs the writes inside the caller's transaction and, when no row matches, throws `seatGone` or `memberHeld`, so the save's handovers roll back. `resolveSeatHandovers` records both facts in each write; the early check stays. `sessions.update.ts` calls `writeSeatHandovers`. Tests in `sessions.seatHandover.int.test.ts`: two guests racing (second refused, first keeps it), benched after the check, removed after the check.
2. **Drag.** `SessionPersonas.svelte` uses the one `player-seat` type for every group. `handleSeatDrag` (`EditSessionForm.svelte`) refuses a drop into another person's group and restores both lists as they were when the drag began. DOM tests for the refused drop and for drag to Benched from two groups. `docs/sessions.md` updated.
3. **Removed guest's seats.** `removeGuest` (`sockets/sessions.ts`) hands the seats the guest seated back to the owner through `handOverSeat`, except those holding the guest's own card. A seat whose person is outside the session shows **Played by** with nothing picked (never an id) and **No one** recovers it, even with no guest left (`EditSessionForm.svelte` `playedByChoice`). Test: remove a guest who took a member, so the seat is the owner's and offered again; the guest's own-card seat is unchanged.
4. **Accessible names.** `Select` gained `accessibleName` (input `aria-label`, trigger "Show … options"). The visible label stays "Played by"; each control reads "Played by, {seat name}" in `SessionPersonas`/`SessionCharacters`. DOM test.
5. **Docs.** The "changes its sheet as its player" clause is removed from `docs/sessions.md`.
