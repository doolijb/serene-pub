**What a lock stops:** no run writes, holds, archives or takes back a locked entry; the automatic Apply and Close out leave it held. A locked scene: no run note, no routine / On-close / Close-out summary or name, no cast drawn from its lines. **Not stopped:** a person's edit, Apply, Take back, Summarize, Compile; scene boundaries (a locked open scene still closes); links and knowledge ties to the row; place stat values.
