## Scope review fix (gs-3a3b-1-fix, 2026-11-17)

**Order and diffs.** `sessions:update` now runs the moves right after the member snapshot, before the character, persona and card-less member diffs. The move's seat is not unseated by those diffs: the character diff keeps a seat made by a move (listed in `characterIds` or not), the card-less member diff skips it, and `isGroup` counts it. A card in `characterIds` is already the AI seat, so nothing is seated twice and no `alreadyAi` follows. The persona diff ignores a `personaIds` entry for a card that a live character seat held when the diff began and that has no live player seat: not an error, not a revive, no ownership check (so a guest's card never throws on the owner's saves). The set is read before the character diff, so one save naming a card in both lists still makes the ★K-2 pair; `sessions:addPersona` (separate event) is unchanged and tested. The list cannot tell "still names it" from "add it", hence the rule.

**What rolls back what.** The pre-check (`resolveSeatMoves`, before any write) rolls back the whole save: owner only, seat gone, no card or member, already AI, holds the turn, a run in progress, genre shape. The same checks repeated inside `moveSeatKind` roll back only the move block's transaction; earlier writes of the save (handovers) stay.

**Payload rule for a form (gs-3a3b-2).** Send the move in `seatMoves`. Leave the moved card out of `personaIds`, or keep it there: it is ignored. `characterIds` may list the moved card or omit it; the AI seat survives both. Do not rely on a `personaIds` entry to bring a player seat back for a card the AI plays: use `sessions:addPersona`. (Comment at the door in `sessions.update.ts`.)

**`maySpeakAsCard`.** The "no person speaks" answer applies only when the session has a removed player seat for the card, a live character seat holds it, and no player seat is live. A card that never had a player seat here keeps the earlier answer (its owner may send as it: a pipeline `create-message` with a card speaker, `sendPersonaMessage`). Test 16.
