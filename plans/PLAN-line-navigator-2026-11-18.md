## 1. The model

Per line: `id | null`, `name`, `forkedFromBranchId`, fork Y/M/D, `present`, `first`, `pointCount`, `points: { year, month, day, kind: "history" | "amendment" | "version", label }[]` (one per distinct date, capped at 200), `sessions: { id, name }[]` (only the asker's own, from `sessions.lorebook_branch_id`); top level `calendar`. Re-sent where `lorebooks:lines` is today (`s:sockets/amendments.ts` ~2211 and its rename / delete neighbours) and after `lorebooks:setClock` (`lorebookStoryTime.ts` ~286).
**Module `sh:lorebooks/lineLanes.ts`** (pure): `lineLanes(spans) → { lanes, axis, forks }`. Axis: every distinct date sorted with `compareDates`, neighbours spaced by their `dateValue` difference clamped between a minimum and a maximum gap. Span: fork date to the later of present and last point; main from its first point; an undated fork starts at its parent's present. Lane rule: main lane 0; others in order of fork date then id; each takes the lowest lane beyond its parent's whose last occupant ended before it starts. Deterministic (total sort key; no measuring). Dots closer than a minimum distance merge into a counted dot. Output is abstract `(along, lane)`: vertical and horizontal come from the same output.

## 6. Extend a line (NX1)
A line's present is its stored clock, else its newest history day. Extending could (1) move the present, writing nothing (`lorebooks:setClock` does it already); (2) start a day: an empty history entry at the new date; (3) fork from the end: a new line (what the line chip's Fork already does). Backwards differs: a fork's past is its parent, so extending it backwards would move its fork date and change what it inherits; only main can grow backwards, by gaining an earlier day. Recommended: forwards = move the present; backwards, on main only = start a day at the earlier date; on a fork the backward tail is absent; owner only; absent on a provided lorebook.

2. **The read is small, asked for when the dialog opens, and scoped.** `lorebooks:lineSpans` returns only what the client lacks: per line `id`, `points` (dated columns plus `branch_id` selected in SQL, never `fields`), `pointCount`, and the asker's own `sessions` (shown in the list as *Your session ‹name› reads this line*); names, fork points, presents and the calendar are joined on the client from `BookTime`. It is requested when the dialog opens, not in `BookTime.listen`, and is not re-sent on other writes (no edits to `amendments.ts` or `setClock`). The reply carries `lorebookId` at the top level; the event is added to `sh:sockets/interest.ts` (`SCOPED_EVENTS` ~274, `GATED_EVENTS` ~1025) and `c:sockets/typedSocket.ts` (~4094). The whole reply is capped (points across all lines), not only each line. A lorebook with its timeline off returns no points. `first` is not in nav-1a.
3. **Spans and columns follow how a line is read** (nav-1b): a span starts at the cut `lineOf` gives (`sh:lorebooks/lineReading.ts` ~286, the earliest cut along the chain), and runs from the earliest to the latest of start, present and points (a present before the fork date, or a child forked before its parent existed, cannot give an end before a start). A line with nothing dated gets no position on the axis and is listed in an **Undated** group; no placeholder date ever. Equal dates: a column is free for a line that starts on the date its last occupant ended. Dots merge in the view at the drawn scale, never in the model. A named test case for each.
4. **Words.** Bare "lane" means a processing queue (NOMENCLATURE ~896) and "channel lane" a session's: the drawing's unit is a **drawing column** (`lineColumns.ts`, `lineColumns()`); never "lane" in code, prose or copy. §25 line.
5. **Long lists.** Lines have no cap: the dialog follows STYLE-GUIDE §6.6 for long lists (the find row with *N of M*, the chosen value above the list), not type-to-find alone. `onlyRenderVisibleElements` is not something `PipelineMap` sets today; nav-1b decides it by measuring.

Backwards: only on the main line, by starting an empty day at the earlier date". nav-1d builds Extend so: forwards = `lorebooks:setClock` to the chosen date; backwards, on the main line only = one empty history day at the earlier date through the existing history-day writer; a fork has no backward tail; the lorebook's owner only; absent on a provided lorebook.
