## The review row (★ copy)
Its own row kind `knowledge-tie` with `tieId` (not a link row; `standaloneLinksOf` keeps its `isNull(knowledgeKind)` filter). Listed only when: a play kind (*was told*, *has been there*); active; on no branch; in the notes; its member and entry are both the lorebook's; no active hand tie stands for the pair in the lorebook. Sentences: *‹member› was told about ‹entry›* / *‹member› has been to ‹entry›*. In the applied list: *‹sentence› · knowledge*; counts as a change. A hand tie set in the notes is never offered.

## The move function (`lorebooks/entryKnowledge.ts`)
- `moveKnowledgeTieToBook` → moved | superseded | twin | gone: re-checks the lorebook belongs to whoever applies and that member and entry are still the lorebook's; a hand tie for the pair → the tie is deleted (superseded); a live tie of the same kind, line and day already in the lorebook → deleted (twin; a resolved twin does not count); otherwise moves the same row.
- `dropSupersededKnowledgeTies` runs on every Apply, selected or not. `handTieInBook` is read-only.
- Dating through one helper shared with links (`dayInLorebook`); the line is the session's line after the fork; the `apply` record carries `cause.moved` and `knowledgeTie`.

## Refusals
A superseded tie gets no row: deleted and counted in `supersededTies`; a duplicate in `droppedTies`. Take back refuses with ★ *The knowledge tie "‹sentence›" is no longer in the lorebook, so this can't be taken back.*

## Back and Take back
Before Apply, Back removes the tie. After Apply, Back is refused by the settle line, or, when an earlier item still waits, it runs and the tie stays in the lorebook (tested). Take back returns the tie to the notes with its dating (the existing link loop). Ties on an entry made in the notes still go with that entry, hand ties included (now pinned).

## The cap and automatic Apply
Ties still waiting hold back Apply's settle line; superseded ties never do. The scene-close Apply carries ties in range; it runs only when the session's owner owns the lorebook and its write setting in force is Full, so a tie waits there only if it was written before the book became Full.

## Shared lorebook
As links: everyone in the session sees the row; only the lorebook's owner can Apply it (`notOwner` otherwise); never applies by itself.

## Receipt (★)
*Kept in this session's notes; Apply offers it to the lorebook.* / *Kept in this session's notes with ‹entry›; it goes to the lorebook when ‹entry› is applied.* / *Kept in this session's notes: ‹member› is in this session's cast only.* / none when the notes are the session's whole lorebook.
