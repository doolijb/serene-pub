## What switched (file → what it now reads)

### New: `src/lib/client/components/sessionPage/projections/seatIdentity.ts`
A leaf module with no imports. There is one seat order, mirroring the server's `seatOf`: live seats first, then character seats before player seats, then position, then id, with envoy seats skipped.
- **`seatRefOf(seat, form)`:** the seat's own `ref` from the host. Without one, ⏳ today's form: `character:<card>`, or `player-seat:<id>` for a card-less player seat.
- **`seatOfRef(session, ref)`:** finds the seat a ref names, without parsing it. It tries the seat's own ref, then a player seat's `player-seat:<id>`, then a seat's `seated-member:<castMemberId>`.
- **`seatOfCastMember(session, castMemberId, prefer?)`:** the seat holding a cast member.
- **`seatOfMessage(session, msg)`:** the seat a line came from. It uses the line's `castMemberId` within the table its card column points to, then the card column itself, then a stamped `player-seat:` speaker.
- **`messageRefOf(session, msg)`:** the host `ref` of the seat the line's card column finds. Without one, ⏳ the line's own card columns. A card-less player seat's line stays null (★S3W-1).
- **`castEntryOf(byId, ref)` and `castEntryOfMember(byId, castMemberId)`:** look up a `state.cast.byId` entry by its `ref`, or by its `seated-member:<castMemberId>`. ⏳ Today's key (`player_seat:<id>` or the bare card id) is used only while **no** entry carries a `ref`; otherwise a card id could land on a member's key and name the wrong person.
- **Types:** `PayloadSeat`, `SeatsPayload`, `SeatFound`, `SeatMessage`, `CastById`.
